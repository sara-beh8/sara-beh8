# Sara Beheyt

**Growth marketer — demand generation, lifecycle & data analytics for SaaS, with a growing focus on companies that are good for people and the planet.**

How I work:

1. **Start with what's already there.** I assess the technologies in place and what they can do before adding anything new.
2. **Make sure the data is clean and legitimate.** When it isn't, I fix that first, because automating on bad data only scales the mess.
3. **Automate what can be automated.** The repetitive side of growth becomes a system I supervise, so the focus goes to creative work and the opportunities repetition hides.

Ten years across the full funnel in SaaS, often joining when the data foundations still needed building and leaving them clean, documented and automated. I build fully automated systems that connect AI, marketing tooling and data engines (for example Claude + Cargo + Customer.io on top of BigQuery), from account research to personalized outreach to attribution.

The repositories here document the systems side of that work: governed campaign operations, AI-assisted build pipelines, and reliable measurement.

🔗 **Case studies with results:** [sara-beh8.github.io](https://sara-beh8.github.io) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sara-beheyt/)

| Repo | What it is |

| [**lifecycle-campaign-skills**](https://github.com/sara-beh8/lifecycle-campaign-skills) | A lifecycle campaign build pipeline as AI skills: naming & tracking → design → segment → copy → HTML → QA |

The three systems repos fit together: **standards** define the names and UTMs → the **pipeline** builds every campaign with them → the **dashboard** attributes results on a single join key.

All examples are generalized, using a fictional company and synthetic data — the architecture and methods are real; the configurations are illustrative. Once I understand a company's data, I can use these patterns to rebuild the same flows on its own stack with AI, quickly.
